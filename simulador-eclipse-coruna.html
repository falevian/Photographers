La corona a simular

The corona to simulate

Radio de la corona en radios solares. 2 R es la corona interior brillante; 4 R una captura fotográfica típica; 7–8 R los penachos largos en exposiciones prolongadas. En 2026 el Sol está cerca del máximo de actividad: corona casi simétrica y con penachos en todas las latitudes.
Radius of the corona in solar radii. 2 R is the bright inner corona; 4 R a typical photographic capture; 7–8 R the long streamers in extended exposures. In 2026 the Sun is near activity maximum: an almost symmetric corona with streamers at all latitudes.

The time comes from Espenak's formula: t = f² / (ISO · 2^Q). Each unit of Q is one stop of light.

The Baader ND 3.8 is photographic only: never observe or focus by eye through it or through the optical viewfinder. To look, use ND 5.0.

Simulator created by Rafael Vida. Local circumstances: municipal table of the Spanish Instituto Geográfico Nacional (contacts to the second, solar altitude and azimuth, sunset). Model: solar disc 0.5261° for a distance of 1.0133 AU, eclipse magnitude 1.03 after the IGN, centre separation fitted to the C1 and C2 instants of the chosen site, field of view 2·arctan(side / 2·focal length), exposure after Fred Espenak's guide t = f² / (ISO · 2^Q), extinction from the Kasten and Young air mass recomputed for every instant, Bennett refraction, sidereal drift 15.04″/s·cos δ. An estimate for planning; it is no substitute for a real test with your own kit.

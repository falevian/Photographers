"median" is the median, not the mean: an occasional 400 mm does not move the row. "% < 1/f" is the share of photographs slower than the camera-shake threshold 1/feq —1/(2·feq) on the 60 Mpx bodies, M11 and Q3—, leaving out anything longer than a second, which is taken to be on a tripod. "from", "last" and "last 12 m" are counted on DateTimeOriginal, with the most recent photograph of the archive as the present.

Método y límites
Method and limits

El lector EXIF es propio y funciona sin conexión: en los JPEG busca el segmento APP1 con cabecera Exif y dentro lee el TIFF; los DNG y TIFF son un TIFF desde el primer byte y entran por el mismo camino. Acepta los dos órdenes de byte (II y MM). Etiquetas leídas: Make, Model, Orientation, ExposureTime, FNumber, ISO, RecommendedExposureIndex, ISOSpeed, DateTimeOriginal, Flash, FocalLength, FocalLengthIn35mmFilm y LensModel. Nada más. ISOSpeed y RecommendedExposureIndex solo se usan cuando ISO llega saturado a 65 535, que es lo que ocurre por encima de esa cifra al ir en un entero de 16 bits: sin ellas, los ISO 102 400 y 204 800 de la SL3-S se leerían como 51 200.

The EXIF reader is home-made and works offline: in JPEGs it looks for the APP1 segment with the Exif header and reads the TIFF inside; DNG and TIFF files are a TIFF from the very first byte and take the same path. Both byte orders (II and MM) are accepted. Tags read: Make, Model, Orientation, ExposureTime, FNumber, ISO, RecommendedExposureIndex, ISOSpeed, DateTimeOriginal, Flash, FocalLength, FocalLengthIn35mmFilm and LensModel. Nothing else. ISOSpeed and RecommendedExposureIndex are used only when ISO arrives saturated at 65,535, which is what happens above that figure in a 16-bit integer: without them the SL3-S's ISO 102,400 and 204,800 would read as 51,200.

De cada archivo se leen solo los primeros 256 KB (File.slice): el EXIF vive al principio, así que mil fotos caben en segundos y en poca memoria. Límites: HEIC y HEIF no se intentan y cuentan como no legibles; los vídeos y el resto de archivos se ignoran. Una foto sin EXIF se cuenta y se sigue adelante. Y todo ocurre en el navegador: no hay red implicada en ningún paso.

Only the first 256 KB of each file are read (File.slice): the EXIF lives at the start, so a thousand photographs fit in seconds and in very little memory. Limits: HEIC and HEIF are not attempted and count as unreadable; videos and every other file are ignored. A photograph without EXIF is counted and the reading moves on. And everything happens in the browser: no network is involved at any step.

Un aviso sobre los objetivos M: el cuerpo solo escribe LensModel si lee el código de seis bits de la montura o si le has dicho a mano qué objetivo llevas. Todo lo que no lleve código y no esté fijado en el menú de la M11 cae en un único saco, «objetivo sin identificar», y arrastra ahí sus focales y sus diafragmas. Si tu saco pesa, la solución está en la cámara, no aquí: fijar el objetivo a mano antes de disparar.

A warning about M lenses: the body only writes LensModel if it reads the six-bit code on the mount or if you have told it by hand which lens is fitted. Anything without a code and not set in the M11 menu falls into a single bucket, "unidentified lens", dragging its focal lengths and apertures in with it. If that bucket is heavy, the fix is in the camera, not here: set the lens by hand before shooting.
